## About us

Amrith Sagar Catering has fed Karnataka's celebrations for three generations, since 1956, and is now run by Nitish, the founders' grandson.

**How it began (1956).** Nitish's grandparents started cooking for weddings and temple festivals in old Bengaluru from a small kitchen in Basavanagudi. Their first big order was a 300-guest wedding lunch served on banana leaves. Guests talked about the bisi bele bath and the holige for weeks, and the next orders came by word of mouth.

**Growing with the city (1970s–1990s).** The second generation added North Indian dishes as Bengaluru grew more cosmopolitan, took on large weddings across south Karnataka, and built a trained team of cooks and servers who stayed with the family for decades.

**Today.** Under Nitish, Amrith Sagar serves everything from a 50-guest housewarming breakfast to a 3,000-guest wedding, and from a traditional South Indian bale ele oota to Italian, Japanese and international dessert menus. Recipes from 1956 are still cooked the same way; new cuisines are added with specialist chefs.

**What we are known for**

- 70 years of word-of-mouth reputation across Karnataka
- Authentic Karnataka and South Indian cooking, especially traditional wedding meals
- Expertise across cuisines: South Indian, North Indian, Italian, Japanese, world desserts and Indian sweets
- Complete "just pay and enjoy" service: food, staff, plates, utensils, setup and cleaning are all on us
- Open 365 days a year, including festivals

**Our promise:** fresh food cooked the same day, served hot, on time, by our own trained staff.

## Company policies

Book at least 15 days ahead, confirm with a 40% advance, and cancel at least 20 days before the event (20% of the order value is charged); the rules below are what the agent quotes to customers.

### 1. Booking

- Minimum notice: **15 days** before the event (20 days for Zone 3 and Zone 4 locations).
- A booking made by phone or by our AI assistant is a **reservation** held under the customer's name for **48 hours**. We send a secure payment link for the 40% advance to the customer's mobile; the booking is confirmed as soon as the advance is paid, and the confirmation is sent there.
- One event date can hold a limited number of events (kitchen capacity: up to 5,000 plates per day). Dates are confirmed first come, first served.

### 2. Payment

- **Advance:** 40% of the estimated order value to confirm the booking.
- **Balance:** remaining 60% at least 3 days before the event, adjusted for the final guest count.
- **Payment modes:** the secure payment link we send (UPI, cards, net banking), bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS/RTGS), card at our office, or cheque (must clear before the event). No cash above ₹2 lakh.
- A GST invoice is issued for every order.

### 3. Cancellation and date changes

- Cancellation must be made **at least 20 days before** the event, in writing (WhatsApp or email) or by calling 9108820437.
- A **cancellation charge of 20% of the order value** applies; the rest of the advance is refunded within 7 working days.
- Cancellations made less than 20 days before the event: the full advance is non-refundable.
- **Date change:** one free date change if requested 20 or more days before the event, subject to availability on the new date. Later changes are treated as a cancellation.

### 4. Guest count and menu changes

- The final guest count is due **5 days** before the event and is the minimum billed.
- On the day, up to 10% more guests are served and billed per plate; more than that depends on food available.
- Menu changes are accepted up to **7 days** before the event.

### 5. Tasting

- Free tasting for orders of 300 or more guests (up to 4 people) at our Basavanagudi kitchen, by appointment.
- For smaller orders, tasting costs ₹1,500, adjusted against the final bill if the order is confirmed.

### 6. Food quality and safety

- FSSAI-licensed central kitchen; licence number printed on every invoice.
- All food is cooked fresh on the day of the event; nothing is reheated from previous events.
- Staff wear uniforms, caps and gloves; food is transported in sealed, hot-holding containers.
- **Allergies:** dishes can be labelled and made without common allergens on request, but our kitchen handles nuts, dairy, gluten and seafood, so we cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment. Customers must tell us about serious allergies when booking.

### 7. Leftover food

- Leftovers can be packed for the host in containers the host provides.
- With the host's permission, safe surplus food is donated the same night through our food-donation partner.

### 8. Venue requirements

- A covered area for the serving counters and a space for our kitchen team near the venue.
- Water and electricity supply; access to the venue at least 4 hours before serving time.
- Venue booking, decoration, tables and chairs for guests, and permissions are the host's responsibility.

### 9. Service time and staff

- Standard service window: 3 hours per meal. Extension: ₹5,000 per extra hour.
- Standard staffing: 1 server per 25 guests, plus counter staff and a service supervisor.
- Any damage to the venue caused by our staff is our responsibility. Loss of or damage to our equipment caused by guests is charged at cost.

### 10. Calls, transcripts and privacy

- Some calls are made or answered by our AI assistant, which says so at the start of the call.
- Calls are not audio-recorded. The conversation is transcribed as text for quality and to note booking details, and the assistant says so at the start. Customer details are used only for their order and service follow-up and are never shared or sold.

**Do you give discounts?** Only volume discounts: 5% for 500–999 guests and 8% for 1,000 guests or more.

**When do I pay the balance?** The remaining 60% is due at least 3 days before the event.

**How can I pay?** Through the secure payment link we send (UPI, cards, net banking), bank transfer, card at our office, or cheque that clears before the event.

**Can I pay the advance on this call?** No. Payments are not taken on calls. We send a secure payment link to your mobile, and you get the confirmation there once you pay.

### Locations and others

**Do you cater outside Bengaluru?** Yes, all over Karnataka. Mysuru, Mandya, Tumakuru and nearby cities have a flat travel charge; other districts are quoted per event.

**Do you provide alcohol?** We provide bar service (bartenders, glassware, ice and mixers), but the host supplies the liquor and the event liquor licence. Alcohol is served only to guests aged 21 and above.

**Do you cater corporate events and offices?** Yes: office parties, conferences, annual days and boxed meals from 50 boxes.

### What the agent can do

- Answer questions about services, menus, packages, prices, policies and service areas (knowledge-base search).
- Calculate a price estimate with the `calculate_quote` tool (package or custom menu, guests, children, add-ons, zone). Prices are never calculated by the LLM itself.
- Check whether a date is available with `check_availability`.
- Reserve a date with `create_tentative_booking` (name, phone, event type, date, venue area, guests, package or custom menu notes): held 48 hours, added to the business calendar, payment link for the 40% advance sent to the customer.
- Log a callback for the team with `schedule_callback` (reason, preferred time).
- Run outbound campaign conversations and record the answers.

### Scope limits (current version)

- **No payments on calls.** The agent never collects, accepts or verifies payment, card, UPI or bank details. If asked: "Payments are not taken on calls. I've sent a secure payment link to your mobile."
- **Reservations, not confirmations.** The agent says "reserved under your name" and never says a booking is confirmed. Confirmation happens automatically when the 40% advance is paid through the link (or manually by staff).
- **No discounts or special prices** beyond the published volume discounts (5% for 500–999 guests, 8% for 1,000+).
- **No exceptions to policies** (15-day notice, 20-day cancellation, 20% charge). Requests for exceptions are logged as callbacks for the owner.
- **No medical or allergy guarantees.** For serious allergies the agent explains the kitchen policy and logs a callback.
- **No legal advice on liquor licences**; it only explains that the host obtains the event licence from the Karnataka Excise Department.
- **Future versions** may add WhatsApp messages and balance-payment reminder calls.

### Hand over to a human (schedule a callback) when

- The customer is upset or complaining about a past event.
- The order is above 1,000 guests, outside Karnataka, or needs a custom quote (Zone 4 travel).
- The customer asks for an exception, a discount, or something not in the knowledge base.
- The customer asks to speak to a person, or to Nitish.
- The same question fails twice (the agent could not understand or answer).

### Always

- Say in the opening that it is an AI assistant, that we keep a written record of the call for quality, and ask which language the customer is comfortable with.
- Use exact figures from the tools and knowledge base; if something is unknown, say "I'll have our team confirm that" instead of guessing.
- Repeat key details back before creating a tentative booking (date, guests, package, area).
- Keep the conversation on catering; politely steer back after small talk.

## Call scripts and data captured

Three outbound campaigns and one inbound flow; each lists the questions the agent must cover (asked naturally, in any order, with follow-ups) and the fields written to Google Sheets after the call. The machine-readable version that the code uses is `backend/data/campaign_types.json`; keep the two in sync.

### Campaign 1: Post-event feedback

Called 1–3 days after the event.

1. Confirm the person and the event ("your daughter's wedding reception on Sunday at Jayanagar").
2. How was the food overall? (rating 1–5)
3. Was the quantity enough for all guests?
4. How were our staff and service? (rating 1–5)
5. Was the setup on time?
6. Any dish guests especially liked, or anything we should improve?
7. Would you book us again?
8. Would you recommend us to friends or family? If yes and they are happy: ask for a Google review (the team sends the link).

If the customer complains: apologise, note the details, schedule a callback from Nitish. Do not offer refunds or discounts.

**Sheet columns:** call_id, customer_name, phone, event_date, event_type, package, guests, food_rating, quantity_ok, service_rating, on_time, liked_dishes, improvements, complaint (yes/no), complaint_details, would_rebook, would_refer, review_link_requested, callback_needed, summary, language, call_duration.

### Campaign 2: Enquiry follow-up

Called 2–3 days after a customer asked for a quote but did not book.

1. Remind them of their enquiry (event type, date, guests).
2. Ask whether they have any questions about the menu or quote.
3. Answer from the knowledge base; recalculate the quote if guests or package changed.
4. Offer a tasting (free for 300+ guests) or a tentative booking.
5. If not interested, politely ask why (price, date, chose another caterer, event cancelled).

**Sheet columns:** call_id, customer_name, phone, event_type, event_date, guests, package_discussed, quote_amount, questions_asked, outcome (tentative booking / tasting / thinking / not interested), reason_not_interested, callback_needed, summary, language.

### Campaign 3: Pre-event confirmation

Called 5–6 days before the event (before the final guest-count deadline).

1. Confirm the event date, serving time and venue address with landmark.
2. Confirm the final guest count (reminder: this is the minimum billed).
3. Confirm the menu, any changes (allowed until 7 days before; later changes go to the team).
4. Check venue access time (4 hours before serving), water and power.
5. Remind about the balance payment due 3 days before; the team will share payment details (no payment on the call).

**Sheet columns:** call_id, customer_name, phone, event_date, serving_time, venue_address, landmark, final_guest_count, menu_changes, access_time_ok, water_power_ok, balance_reminder_given, callback_needed, summary.

### Inbound calls

1. Greet, disclose AI, ask the preferred language.
2. Understand the need: question, quote, availability, booking, complaint or something else.
3. Answer, quote (tool), check the date (tool), and offer a tentative booking (tool) when the customer is ready.
4. Before ending, repeat the next step: "Your date is reserved for 48 hours; please pay the advance using the link we sent, and your confirmation will come there."

**Sheet columns:** call_id, caller_name, phone, intent, event_type, event_date, guests, area, package_or_menu, quote_amount, tentative_booking_id, callback_needed, callback_reason, summary, language.
